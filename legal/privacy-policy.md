## Information We Collect

- **Account information.** When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your
  email address and (if you choose to share it) your name. We use Sign in with
  Apple's private-relay email if you select that option.
- **Profile & fitness data.** Information you enter during onboarding and use:
  name, age, biological sex, height, weight, activity level, fitness goal, and the
  calorie/macro targets we calculate for you.
- **Health & fitness data (HealthKit).** With your explicit permission, we read
  activity data (such as active energy and steps) and write completed workouts to
  Apple Health. Data read from HealthKit is used only to show your in-app metrics
  and is **never** sold or used for advertising.
- **Purchase data.** If you buy a subscription or in-app purchase, Apple processes
  the transaction; we receive a record that a purchase occurred. We do not receive
  your payment card details.

## How We Use Your Information

- To create and maintain your account.
- To calculate and display your personalized targets, meals, and workouts.
- To sync your data across your devices.
- To respond to support requests and feedback.

## Storage & Security

Your data is stored using **Supabase** (our backend provider) and protected with
industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. We retain your data until you
delete your account.

## Health Data

Health and fitness data obtained through HealthKit is used solely to provide app
functionality. We do not use HealthKit data for advertising or marketing, and we
do not share it with third parties or use it for any purpose other than improving
your health and fitness experience, consistent with Apple's requirements.

## Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from
**Profile → Delete Account** in the App. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

## Third-Party Services

- **Google** (Sign in with Google)
- **Supabase** (authentication and data storage)

Each has its own privacy policy governing data they process.

## Children's Privacy

Formé is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country),
and we do not knowingly collect their data.
